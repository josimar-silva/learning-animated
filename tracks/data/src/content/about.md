Data Animated explains how databases find, split, and spread data with looping animations, one idea at a time. The lessons on indexes and partitioning follow PostgreSQL, and every example comes from one online multiplayer game: its players, their matches, and the database servers that hold them. Every animation is a standalone SVG file that anyone may embed for noncommercial use, with credit, under [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).
